Answers, not a wall of numbers

Understand the battery you have today. Plan for the one you'll need tomorrow.

WhatBattery turns the data macOS already holds into three useful answers: how healthy the battery really is, where the power is going, and how quickly the battery is wearing.

1

How worn is it, really?

System Settings rounds battery health to a whole number, so anything from 99.5% up shows as 100%. WhatBattery works from the raw capacity figures and shows one decimal place, alongside cycle count and Apple's own service condition.

You see wear starting months before a rounded number admits it.

See how true health works →
2

Where is the power going?

See the real watts flowing into or out of the battery, the charger your Mac negotiated, voltage, and temperature. Pro also ranks your own apps by live watts and keeps the day's top power users.

Activity Monitor gives you an energy score. WhatBattery gives you watts and tells you honestly what cannot be attributed to an app.

Explore live power and app usage →
3

When might it need replacing?

Pro builds a long-term health record, measures the wear rate, and projects the window in which your Mac is likely to reach 80% health. If the battery starts ageing faster than its own trend, WhatBattery flags the change.

Not just “91% today”, but a replacement window you can budget around, and silence when the history is too thin to support one.

Common questions

The important details, before you install.

Does WhatBattery change how my Mac charges?

No. It is strictly read-only. It reads battery and power data through Apple's interfaces and never writes to the battery, enforces a charge limit, or changes charging behaviour.

Why is it not in the Mac App Store?

The App Sandbox blocks the low-level IOKit and SMC reads WhatBattery needs. It ships directly as a Developer ID-signed and Apple-notarised app, so it opens without a Gatekeeper warning.

How does the iPhone and iPad reading work?

Trust the Mac once over a cable, then read the device over USB or Wi-Fi using the same connection Finder uses. No app is installed on the device. Read the setup guide.

What does Pro cost?

£9.99 once, for up to two Macs. There is no subscription and purchases have a 14-day money-back guarantee.
