The long view

Know whether 91% is stable or on the way down quickly.

A single health figure has no direction. Pro keeps the history needed to show whether wear is gradual, accelerating, or approaching the point where a replacement deserves planning.

  • Lifetime Analyzer for power, temperature, voltage, and charge
  • Monthly health and cycle history per device
  • Battery runway forecast for Macs
  • Smart alerts for unusual wear and charging conditions
Diagnosis with receipts

See what drained it and how the charger performed.

Apps are ranked in real watts rather than a unitless energy score. Charging sessions preserve the delivered power, heat, and charger capability, so the diagnosis does not disappear as soon as you unplug.

  • Live and historical app power
  • Charge-session history
  • Charger capability verdict
  • Thirty-day charging-habit summary
  • Threshold and wear-driven alerts
All your devices

Bring the iPhone and iPad into the same record.

Read connected-device health over USB or Wi-Fi without installing another app. Pro retains sparse, long-term health and cycle history so the devices do not vanish from view when they disconnect.

  • Health, cycles, temperature, voltage, and live power
  • Current-session graph
  • Long-term monthly health record
  • Mac and connected-device history in one tab
Before it dies

Know the keyboard is going flat days before it does.

A Bluetooth accessory reports a charge level and nothing else: no health, no cycles, no warning. Pro records that level over time and turns it into a forecast, so a dead mouse in the middle of something is a thing you saw coming.

  • Level history per accessory, kept for months
  • How long is left: days for a mouse, listening time for AirPods
  • Roughly how long a full charge lasts
  • AirPods tracked per bud and case
  • A low-battery alert that fires before it dies, and says how long is left
  • Any accessory's level shown in the menu bar

One simple licence

Stripe completes the payment and the licence key is emailed immediately. Paste it into WhatBattery's Licence settings.

Contact support through the GitHub repository and a licence slot can be freed for the replacement.
