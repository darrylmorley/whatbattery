# WhatBattery 1.4.0

A redesign. Same readings, considerably easier to read. macOS 14 and later, Apple Silicon.

## The window looks like a Mac app now

Every tab is built from sections that sit on their own surface, rather than one long scroll of label-and-value rows divided by hairlines. Readings flow across the width instead of hugging the left margin, which is where most of the window was going. Charts lost the dashed grid that competed with the data, and gained a soft fill under the line.

## Two things moved

**Charging habits now lives on the Charging tab**, directly above the charge sessions that are its own evidence. It was a tab away, on This Mac, which meant the summary and the detail behind it were never on screen together.

**The History tab is now called Health.** Three tabs show historical data (app energy over 24 hours, recent charges, long-term health), so the old name did not say which one this was.

## The Apps tab

A live trace of total app power across the top, so you can see whether the machine is settling or winding up rather than only what it is doing this second. App icons, and a bar behind each row scaled to the heaviest, so the ranking reads before you get to the numbers.

## Also

- The charger has its own reading rather than being tacked onto the end of the power figure.
- A battery serial no longer breaks across two lines.
- A single-cell accessory stops printing its level twice.
- Repeating animations respect Reduce Motion.

Read-only as always: WhatBattery never changes how your devices charge.
