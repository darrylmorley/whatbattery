# WhatBattery 1.6.5

Two charging fixes, both worth having. macOS 14 and later, Apple Silicon.

## Charging power was understated by roughly 3x

The figure the battery reports for charging voltage turns out to be per-cell,
not per-pack, so a 65 W charge could show as 23 W. Live readings now come from
the pack's own voltage and current, which are correct on every machine
checked (816 real Macs). Charging sessions recorded before this update keep
their old, lower wattages, so the per-charger comparison will look uneven
until newer sessions replace them. Peak charge in the Lifetime Analyzer will
step up after this ships, for the same reason.

Two guards came with the fix: a reading whose direction contradicts the
charging state is treated as no reading rather than a confident wrong one,
and the thermal-throttling detector's threshold was rescaled to match the
corrected wattages.

## No more phantom charging after you unplug at 100%

Unplug a full MacBook and WhatBattery kept saying it was on power: the
battery's "fully charged" flag stays set after the cable is gone, and the app
trusted it over the cable. The cable now wins. This also fixes the "plugged
in at full too long" alert never re-arming after an unplug, and charging
sessions not closing when you unplug at full.

Read-only as always: WhatBattery never changes how your devices charge.
