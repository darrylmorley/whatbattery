# WhatBattery 1.2

A big release. WhatBattery now shows which apps are using your power, reads the accessory batteries System Settings sees but 1.1 could not, and grew a proper Charging tab. macOS 14 and later, Apple Silicon.

## New

- **Apps tab (Pro).** Which apps are working the chip hardest, live, in real watts rather than a unitless score, plus the top power users over the last day, week or month. Your own apps only, honestly framed: the screen and radios belong to nobody, so nothing here pretends to be a share of the whole battery.
- **Charging tab (Pro).** Every charge recorded: held and peak wattage, duration, how far it filled, and a verdict on each of your chargers.
- **Smart alerts (Pro).** Notifications for the things that actually wear a battery: sitting at 100% for hours, no full cycle in a long while, health dropping faster than this battery's own trend, and running warm while charging. Each fires once per crossing, not every five seconds.
- **Charging habits (Pro).** Thirty days of your charging behaviour summarised, with a pointer when a habit warrants Optimized Charging or a charge limiter.
- **Battery summary for resale or warranty (Pro).** A clean report of health, cycles and lifetime history you can export and hand over.
- **Accessory runtime (Pro).** Time-till-empty estimates for accessories, once a little history has been recorded.
- **History retention (Pro).** Choose how long dense history is kept, from three months to forever.
- **More accessory batteries (free).** Devices that only report battery over Bluetooth Low Energy, Logitech MX mice among them, now show a level matching System Settings.
- **Accessories react immediately (free).** Devices appear in the list when they connect and leave when they disconnect, instead of waiting for the next five-minute poll.
- **Desktop Macs get the power view (free).** No battery to report, but the mains draw is live in the popover and main window, as the CLI has done all along.
- **Menu bar badge choice (free).** Pick what the badge shows, and the icon now follows battery state.

## Fixed

- The text-size slider applies when you let go, so the popover no longer flashes and wanders while you drag.
- History records the actual charging state with every sample, so charging analysis no longer misreads a 0 W moment as plugged in.
- The widget keeps its last reading across restarts and marks it stale rather than presenting old numbers as current.
- A full-codebase review's worth of hardening: safer database writes, stricter concurrency, and a bounded update download.

Read-only as always: WhatBattery never changes how your devices charge.
