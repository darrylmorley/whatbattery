# WhatBattery 1.6.4

Correct battery health for pre-2017 iPhones and iPads. macOS 14 and later, Apple Silicon.

## Older iDevices now report their real battery health

On iPhones and iPads from before 2017 (anything older than the iPhone 8/X generation), the battery lives under a different system node than on modern devices, and that node reports two full-charge figures: the measured one and the battery's rated spec. WhatBattery was reading the spec, so an aged battery looked healthier than it is. One real iPad Pro 10,5" read 82% when the honest figure was 67%.

The evidence came from a user running the `--idevice-raw` diagnostic added in 1.6.3: across two days the spec figure never moved while the measured one drifted, which is exactly how you tell a label from a measurement. WhatBattery now uses the measured figure on that older node. Readings from modern iDevices and from Macs are untouched, they were already using the right key.

If you have an older iPad or iPhone, expect its health figure to drop in this update. That is the number becoming true, not the battery getting worse.
