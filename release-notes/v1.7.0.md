# A tidier, clearer WhatBattery

A design pass across the app, plus a new option for the menu bar popover. macOS 14 and later, Apple Silicon.

## Solid popover background

Settings, Appearance has a new "Solid window background" toggle. Turn it on and the menu bar popover uses a plain window colour instead of the see-through backdrop that picks up your desktop. It's off unless you turn it on.

## The popover

- The popover fits its content, so there's no longer a blank gap above the buttons when the battery is full.
- The buttons at the bottom are labelled: Open, Settings and Quit.
- The mark on the health bar is labelled "80%", Apple's service threshold. The bar in the main window and on the iPhone/iPad tab gets the same label.
- The Power line no longer repeats "fully charged" or "not charging". The Charge line above it already says so.

## Health

- The Health tab opens on Monthly. The battery's own capacity estimate moves a few points from day to day, so the Daily view could look unreliable. Daily is still one click away, and if you had already picked it, it stays.
- A short note explains what the health figure is and why it can differ from the one macOS shows. Hover over the big health figure on This Mac for the same explanation.
- The health chart's scale no longer turns a fraction of a percent of noise into a steep zigzag.

## Charts

- Charts no longer draw a straight line across times when nothing was recorded, such as days when WhatBattery wasn't running. Those stretches show as a faint dashed line.
- Date labels at the edges of charts are no longer cut off.
- The temperature chart follows your °C or °F setting, as do the lifetime temperature figures and the Cells and internals card.

## Clearer numbers and names

- Chargers show as "96W charger" instead of "96W pd charger".
- Watts, temperature ranges and dates read the same way on every tab, for example "10.6 to 32.8°C" and "24 Nov 2026".
- The battery report (PDF, print and `whatbattery --report`) follows your °C or °F setting.

## Smaller things

- The Accessories tab opens on the device seen most recently.
- The Apps tab opens on the first time range that has data.
- On the Sleep tab, runs of short sleeps where nothing happened are grouped into one row.
- Four more wake reasons are shown in plain English, such as "The charger was unplugged" and "A key was pressed".
- Cards inside sections are outlined panels instead of grey boxes.

Read-only as always: WhatBattery never changes how your devices charge.
