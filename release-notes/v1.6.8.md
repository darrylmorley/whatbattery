# No more empty Settings window

A small fix release. macOS 14 and later, Apple Silicon.

## WhatBattery starts quietly again

Version 1.6.7 opened an empty "WhatBattery Settings" window every time it started. It doesn't any more: WhatBattery starts in the menu bar and stays there until you open it.

Pressing Cmd-, with a WhatBattery window in front used to bring up that same empty window. It now opens the real Settings.

## Room above the tabs

In 1.6.7 the tabs in the main window sat right against the title bar. They have a little space above them again.

Read-only as always: WhatBattery never changes how your devices charge.
