# macOS 27 and iPadOS 27 support

WhatBattery lost track of battery health on macOS 27, and couldn't read iPads on iPadOS 27. Both are fixed. macOS 14 and later, Apple Silicon.

## Battery health is back on macOS 27

Apple moved most of the battery's figures in macOS 27, so health showed as unknown and several readings went missing. WhatBattery now reads them from their new home. That covers health, capacity, temperature and the per-cell detail in the Pro "Cells and internals" card. Older versions of macOS read exactly as before: I checked that against 1,141 real Macs on macOS 14 to 26, and every one came out identical.

## iPads on iPadOS 27 read again

The same move happened on iPadOS 27, which made an iPad show as "reported data in a shape not readable by this version". It now reads normally.

## New in Pro: when the battery last recalibrated

The "Cells and internals" card now shows the cycle count at which the gauge last relearned its capacity. A recent relearn is the usual reason health seems to drop overnight. There's also `whatbattery --fields`, which shows where each figure was found, handy if a future macOS moves things again.

Read-only as always: WhatBattery never changes how your devices charge.
