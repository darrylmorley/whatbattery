# WhatBattery 1.2.1

A small fix release. macOS 14 and later, Apple Silicon.

## Fixed

- Charger verdicts are now judged on what the charger advertises, not on how fast a charge happened to run. Your Mac paces its own charging (Optimized Charging, sleep, overnight trickle), so a perfectly good charger loafing through a slow charge could be wrongly flagged as underpowered. A 20W phone brick on a MacBook Pro still gets called out.
- The "X charges faster than Y" comparison line is gone for the same reason: held watts reflect the Mac's pacing, and the data can't carry a speed claim between chargers. The observed per-charger figures stay.
- An adapter label that leads with a number that isn't a wattage can no longer be misread as one.

Read-only as always: WhatBattery never changes how your devices charge.
