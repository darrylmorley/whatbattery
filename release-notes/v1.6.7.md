# iPhone and iPad temperature on iOS 27

A small fix release. macOS 14 and later, Apple Silicon.

## Temperature is back for iPhones and iPads on iOS/iPadOS 27

iOS and iPadOS 27 moved the battery temperature to a different spot, the same move macOS 27 made, so every iPhone and iPad on 27 showed Temperature: Unknown. WhatBattery now reads it from its new home. Devices on iOS 26 and earlier read exactly as before. Thanks for the report on #3.

## A steadier power reading

When the Mac's power controller declines to answer, WhatBattery now notices and falls back to the battery's own figure, instead of taking an empty reply as a real one.

Read-only as always: WhatBattery never changes how your devices charge.
